HELL

Same shape as Nightmare — one decision changes

Decide Do we still need Insights and Spirits?

This is the only real call to make in Hell. Check as a group before Act 1 — if anyone is still missing an Insight or Spirit, we split for NM Countess exactly like above. If everyone's geared, nobody splits and we all just TP hop together, which is much faster.

Farm If yes — keep running NM Countess

Same rhythm as Nightmare: farm between calls, come back for every boss. NM Countess, not Hell Countess — Hell Countess drops higher runes but she's far slower to kill at this gear level.

All 8 If no — everybody TP hops

bArracooN leads exactly as in Nightmare, dropping TPs at champ packs, and the whole group moves as one unit through all five acts.

Boss Act order is unchanged

Andy → Staff / Viper / Rada / Summoner / Duriel → Organs / Trav / Meph → Izzy / Chaos / Diablo → Shenk / Barbs / Anya / Ancients / Baal. Watch your resistances — Hell hits the -100 all res penalty and immunities start mattering.

✔ Reminders that bite people in NM and Hell

  • Bonk buys the pots and TPs. The Sorcs burn through both and shouldn't be town-porting for them.
  • Don't turn the Gidbinn quest in early in Act 3.
  • Shop for the Sorcs at the Duriel window — res, life, mana on boots, gloves and belts.
  • Hell applies a -100 to all resistances. Get everyone to max res before you push it.
  • Come back when called. The teleporter standing at a boss is the most expensive thing in the run.
