Nobody has to tick anything. We only wanted a guide that was easier to follow and got a bit carried away — the checkboxes are here if they're useful mid-run, and completely ignorable if they're not. Checking a step also marks the steps before it done — the split groups are tracked separately, so ticking one never touches another.

Rules for the whole run

  • Clear every champ pack and high-density mob group you pass. Don't walk past XP.
  • Less time in town. Gear comes from killing and looting, not shopping.
  • Level 8: gamble a 3-row belt and buy your Act 1 merc.
  • Call out in voice when you're moving, splitting, or dropping a TP. Nobody guesses.
ACT 1

Den → Tristram → Countess split → Andariel

Target: lvl 12–14 by Andy

Scout Stony Field TP

Zinski (Sorc) and Creamsicles (Druid) run straight for Stony Field and leave a TP there.

Quest Clear the Den of Evil

Everyone else (Bonk, XiLeD88, Augural, Not Pavico, Fkityolo, bArracooN) clears Den. Take the skill point.

TP Bonk portals the group to Stones

As soon as Den is done, Bonk (Barb) drops the TP so the group jumps to Stony Field.

Clear Stony Field + Underground Passage

Group clears Stones and runs Underground Passage. Leave a TP at Stones.

Scout Find Black Marsh, then push toward Andy

Zinski + Creamsicles keep running together to find Black Marsh, drop a TP at the WP, then continue pathing toward the Monastery / Andariel.

Quest Find the Tree of Inifuss

The other six hunt down the Tree. Once found → back to town, turn in the scroll, make Tristram.

Loot Run Tristram — Bonk KEEPS the Leg

Everyone except Zinski and Creamsicles runs Trist together. Bonk holds Wirt's Leg all the way to Cows — do not sell it, do not drop it.

Travel Everyone grabs the Black Marsh WP

Take the WP Zinski + Creamsicles opened. Now we split.

⚔ THIS IS WHERE THE GROUP SPLITS ⚔
1 · Rush to Andy

Path Black Marsh → Tamoe → Monastery → Catacombs. Drop TPs at champ packs. Call out when you hit Outer Cloister and again at Cata 3.

2 · Main-Game Countess

Run Countess in the main game. When bArracooN calls "Outer Cloister," you two can make a new game and run a second Countess.

3 · Duo Countess

Make your own game, run Countess on repeat. Hunting Tal / Ral / Ort / Thul for Spirits + Insight.

4 · Duo Countess

Make your own game, run Countess on repeat. Bonk still has the Leg.

Boss Regroup → Andariel

When bArracooN + Creamsicles reach Catacombs 3, everyone finishes their current Countess run and rejoins the main game. Full 8-man kills Andy.

ACT 2

Sewers · Halls · Viper · Summoner · Duriel

Target: lvl 18–20 by Duriel

Solo Bonk leaps the Sewers

Bonk (Barb) uses Leap to blast through the Sewers, drops a TP at Radament, then joins the Halls team.

TWO TEAMS
Viper Team

Run Dry Hills → Far Oasis → Viper Temple. Drop a TP at Maggot Lair so bArracooN can tele from there at lvl 18. Start clearing Viper.

Halls Team

Run to Halls of the Dead and clear it. Bring the extra Horadric Cubes back to town — everyone needs one.

Loot Clear Radament

Halls team clears Rada next. Grab the extra skill books and bring them to town so everyone gets a point.

All 8 Viper Temple (if not done)

If Viper isn't cleared by the time Halls + Rada are finished, everyone joins bArracooN and Augural for the Amulet pop.

All 8 Summoner — and shop

Everyone runs Summoner together. This is the shopping window — res / life / mana gear, especially for the two Sorcs. Be quick.

Boss Clear the main Tomb → Duriel

Clear the true tomb on the way through, then all 8 kill Duriel.

ACT 3

Level check · Organs · Travincal · Mephisto

Everyone must be lvl 20

Gate Anyone under lvl 20 runs Tombs

If you're not lvl 20, run Tombs until you are. Take a killer with you — Augural (Warlock) or Not Pavico (Sin). Once 20, join the main game and help with organs.

TWO TELEPORTERS OUT FRONT
Path to Travincal

Teleport toward Travincal, dropping TPs at every champ pack for the group to hop.

Organ Run

Teleport for the organs (Eye, Heart, Brain), also dropping TPs at champ packs.

Everyone Else

TP hop between the portals the Sorcs drop and clear the packs. Free XP, no walking.

All 8 Travincal

Everyone runs Trav together. Then bArracooN teleports to Mephisto and the group follows for the kill.

Izual · Chaos Sanctuary · Diablo

+2 skill points from Izual

Rush bArracooN teleports to Izual

Drop a TP on Izual, then keep going to the River of Flame WP.

Boss Everyone else kills Izual

Take the TP, kill Izzy, take the +2 skill points.

All 8 Full Chaos Sanctuary clear → Diablo

bArracooN drops a TP at the entrance to Chaos. We run all of Chaos — every seal, every pack — then Diablo.

ACT 5

Shenk · Barbs · Anya · Ancients · Baal

Target: lvl 24+ at Baal

Rush Shenk WP TP, then keep moving

bArracooN heads to Shenk's WP, drops a TP, and immediately keeps teleporting onward.

Clear Everyone else kills Shenk

Take the WP + TP and clear Shenk and the surrounding packs.

RESCUE THE BARBARIANS
Finds the Barbs

Zinski runs / teles Frigid Highlands to locate the Barbarian cages and drops TPs.

Everyone Else

TP hop to Zinski's portals and clear out the Barb rescue.

Quest Anya

bArracooN drops a TP at the Anya entrance so Zinski can teleport straight to her; everyone else clears in. Take the +1 all res scroll.

Boss Ancients → Throne Room → Baal

bArracooN continues to Ancients, we all do the fight, then he teles through Worldstone to the Throne. All 8 for Baal.

✔ After Baal — before Nightmare

  • Bonk opens Cows with Wirt's Leg + Tome of Town Portal in the cube. Run Cows until everyone is lvl 25.
  • LOOK FOR 4-SOCKET BASES: white swords and polearms.
  • Plain Broadswords and Long Swords from Arreat Plateau onward in Act 5 can roll 4 sockets.
  • Crystal Swords and Polearms (not Bardiche) from Arreat Plateau up to Worldstone also roll 4 sockets.
  • At lvl 25 → Normal Baal runs until everyone is lvl 30–40, then move to Nightmare.
